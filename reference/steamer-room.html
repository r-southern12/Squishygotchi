Caring is your job

  1. Needs drain all the time, even while you're away. Pinky can scrape by on its own, up to about half, but only you can fill a need.
  2. Tap furniture to send Pinky there: the bed to nap (lamp off for a full rest), the bath or shower to wash, the ball to play. Tap the stove to pick a recipe.
  3. While it's in the bath, rub Pinky with your finger to scrub it clean faster.
  4. Neglect shows in stages: droopy, flat, then grey. If a need stays empty too long, Pinky dies and a tombstone is left.

Room and items

  1. Drag to turn the room. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  2. Flick the ball to fire it around the room. Pinky chases it.
  3. Decor adds Comfort, which slows how fast needs drain. Three pieces from one style give a set bonus.
  4. Plants wilt unless Pinky waters them. Tap the plant.
  5. The pencil opens Arrange. Drag items anywhere, place things from Storage, and use walls, screens and the shower to divide the room.
  6. In Arrange, use + / − to zoom and the tilt button to switch between top-down and angled. Drag empty floor to turn and tilt, pinch to zoom, and use two fingers to pan (scroll wheel on desktop).

Earning and spending

  • Care tasks (checklist button) pay coins. Finish three for a free steamer.
  • While your squishy is Happy it earns a trickle of coins. Comfort makes the trickle bigger.
  • The shop (bag button, or tap your coins) sells steamers, snacks, ingredients, tools, tool repairs and extra furniture.
  • The room starts small, with one of each item type. Steamers mostly unlock new skins for those types, which you swap in Arrange. Decor is limited by room size. Expand room in Arrange once you have enough squishies and coins.

Kitchen

  • Meals are cooked from ingredients using tools, and the tool appears on the stove while it cooks. Plain congee is always free but only fills hunger to 60%.
  • Each recipe levels up the more you cook it (5 stars), filling more each time.
  • Tools wear out with use and can be repaired or replaced in the shop. Unlock tool skins in steamers and equip them in the catalogue.
  • The pantry cupboard is for quick snacks only (crackers, apple, mochi). Snacks are cheap in the shop.

Catalogue

  • 24 room styles, 19 item types each, plus squishy finishes, kitchen tools, pantry ingredients and steamer skins.
  • Pick a style and tap Preview in my room to see your whole room restyled.

Unboxing

  • Steamers give furniture, tools, ingredients or squishies. A duplicate of your favourite grows it. Duplicate furniture turns into coins.
